Siyyarte — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 June 2026 · Contact: support@bkenaan.dev

Siyyarte ("the app") is a car-maintenance tracker and reminder app for drivers in Lebanon, operated by Bassel Kenaan ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data — your email address or phone number (and name/profile photo if you sign in with Google), used solely to create and secure your account.
  • Profile data — display name, city, and preferred currency, used to personalise the app.
  • Vehicle & service data — vehicles you add (make, model, year, VIN, plate, odometer), service records, costs, and reminders you create.
  • Photos you choose to upload — service invoices, odometer photos, and vehicle registration cards (دفتر السيارة). These are stored privately in your account and are never visible to other users.
  • Push notification token — a device token used to deliver maintenance reminders you asked for.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing history, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.

How photos are processed (AI scanning)

If you use the optional "scan" features, the photo you select is sent securely to our server and processed by an AI service (Anthropic Claude) to extract text such as the service type, date, amount, odometer reading, or — for registration cards — the holder name and chassis number (VIN). The extracted text is shown to you for confirmation and saved with your records. Photos are processed for this purpose only.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) under your own account, protected by security rules so only you can access it. Database servers are located in the Gulf region (Doha); uploaded photos are stored in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Ads and purchases

  • Ads — the free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Subscriptions — Siyyarte Plus purchases are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat (a purchase-management service). We never see your payment card details.

Vehicle ownership transfers

Each car can be registered in only one Siyyarte account. If a buyer requests a transfer of a car registered to you, we send a confirmation email to your account email containing the requester's name or email. The car moves to the buyer's account only if you approve.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account (and all data with it) at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. You can also export your data as a PDF from the app before deleting.

Children

Siyyarte is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the effective date above.
