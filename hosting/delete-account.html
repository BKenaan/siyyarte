Delete your Siyyarte account

App: Siyyarte (com.garagi.garagi) · Developer: Bassel Kenaan

Delete everything from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Siyyarte and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and all associated data: profile, vehicles, service history, reminders, uploaded photos (invoices and registration cards), and push-notification tokens. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Tip: if you want a copy of your records first, export them from Settings → Export my data (Siyyarte Plus) or request a free copy by emailing support@bkenaan.dev.

Delete by request

If you can no longer access the app, email support@bkenaan.dev from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Siyyarte account". We verify ownership and delete the account and all data within 30 days.

Delete specific data without closing your account

  • Vehicles — open the vehicle → menu → Delete vehicle (removes its history and photos too).
  • Service records — open the record in History and delete it.
  • Anything else — email support@bkenaan.dev.

See also our privacy policy.
